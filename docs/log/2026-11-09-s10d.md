# S10d — user rulings from the backend closing audit and the disconnect policy review (2026-11-09)

## Goal

Implement the user's rulings on the audit items that needed a direction, and on the disconnect policy review that followed (does each of Standard, Deferred and NoAction still work as intended). Reducer, helper and view logic: no table change, no publish.

## Decisions implemented

- **A86**: a timer auto-pick of a real character is priced like a manual pick; `EMPTY` stays free.
- **A87**: mirror picks, once per team; `EMPTY` exempt.
- **A88**: a team-seated tournament referee keeps score adjustments and the staff close (no code; contract note).
- **A89**: tournament staff with the bracket permission manage tournament-linked calendar events.
- **A90**: `update_calendar_event` enforces the 40-active cap.
- **A91**: `defer_match` needs a seated player away past the grace or with a spent pool (exclusive spectator referee exempt); `Deferred` only for tournaments; the automatic park at an empty pool is retired; parked lobbies are cleared by hand.
- **A92**: `start_draft` needs every seated player online; grace 30 to 600; `NoAction` for casual lobbies only; away and abandoned players cannot concede, claim or defer; the 15 s transfer check runs in Waiting for a host or referee; eligibility uses the live pool; `client_connected` lifts an auto-pause like `join_lobby`; the manual-pause case is accepted as is; `view_my_lobby_members.disconnectActionAt`.

## Findings and hand-offs

- **Open for the user (review F4): the host role in Waiting.** As decided in A92(5), a host who drops for 15 s in Waiting loses the host role as well as the referee flag. In a tournament lobby created by staff, the role goes to the first online player and nothing returns it. Recommendation put to the user: move the referee flag only.
- **A86 prices on the team** (review F5): the pool is the union of the acting team's selected accounts and the eidolon is the team's highest owned level. Same as a manual pick in 1v1; in 2v2 and above it can draw a teammate's character or a higher level, never a cheaper one. Orchestrator reading of "the acting team's account"; consistent with D-I-01/02.
- **Residual of A92(1)** (review F3): a row with `isOnline = false` whose user has a live presence on another identity passes the start gate unstamped. Narrow; noted in a code comment.
- **No staff bypass of the start gate**: staff unseat or kick an offline player to start.
- **Rows written before this step** keep disallowed values (casual `Deferred`, tournament `NoAction`, out-of-range grace) until their next settings write; gone with the S11 clear-publish.
- **Presets may store Ranked with `NoAction`**; `create_lobby` rejects it when used.
- Parked standalone lobbies now hold a Submitted Ranked record; a Pending record parks only through a tournament defer.
- Deviations from a decision row: none.
