# S14.3a: the team builder page core (2026-11-16)

## Goal

Port the team builder (`/teambuilder`) from the frozen mockup onto real data, first of four sub-steps (S14.3b chart and Auction simulator, S14.3s backend, S14.3c loadouts, share link, screenshot export, footer): the Modes card, the four roster slots with the mockup's slot card, the light cone search, the cost total with pairs and the missing-row warning, the character pool, a team in the address and in browser storage, swapping slots by pointer, touch and keyboard. Test tasks T-S14.3a, T-S14.3a-fix and T-S14.3a-fix2. No backend change, no new subscription, nothing deployed.

## Decisions implemented

- **A190** (team builder rulings): cost sets, draft and game modes as on the costs page, default AS (item 9); default eidolons E6 for 4-stars and Trailblazers, E0 otherwise (item 4); a missing cost row counts 0 with "n/a" and a warning (item 6); swap by handle with drag, click and keyboard (item 8); phones: picking variant A, the sheet (item 13, B as a patch); game mode variant A, the tiles (item 14, B as a patch).
- **A191** (blank character image served by the app; rulings on empty slots).
- The frontend rules A13, A49 and A49a, A160 to A173, A183 and A184 as on the costs page.

## What exists

- `/teambuilder` inside the shell, lazy chunk: intro (`PageIntro`), Modes (Cost Set, Draft Mode, Game Mode tiles), Active Roster (four 3:4 slot cards, pairs strip, Clear Team), Cost Breakdown (total, warning, footer line), Character Pool (the costs page's filter row, toggle cards), the phone sheet.
- The slot card as the mockup's "reference-style slot display": portrait, element and path icons, cost pill (split on hover or keyboard focus, link icon in an active pair), the cone art in the slanted shape, the Eidolon and Superimposition pill, the search bar along the bottom edge, Remove and the move handle.
- The packed team text, version 1 (`shared/teambuilder/teamCode.ts`): share link, browser draft and the future loadout column use one strict, total parser; per-slot Auction bids are in version 1.
- Browser storage: one draft; the previous team kept when a link replaces a different one, with Restore and Dismiss; an unreadable stored draft is left alone.
- Cost rules in `shared/teambuilder/teamCost.ts` and `shared/scoring/auction.ts`, the module's order (pinned by a test).
- Shared pieces: `components/PageIntro`, `components/FilterSearch`, `lib/rosterSearch.ts`, `lib/rosterModel.ts`, `lib/useAnnouncement.ts`, `stdb/useCustomCostSet.ts` (the costs page uses them through re-exports).

Details per part: `docs/frontend/pages.md`, "Team builder (S14.3a)".
