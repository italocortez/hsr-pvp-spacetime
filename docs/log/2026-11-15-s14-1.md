# S14.1: the app shell (2026-11-15)

## Goal

The frame every page lives in: navigation bar, account area, reconnecting notice, keyboard and screen-reader behaviour after navigation, and a first paint that does not wait for the SpacetimeDB client. Test task T-SHELL. Nothing deployed, no backend change.

## Decisions implemented

A13 (one responsive tree), A49 (CSS Modules, tokens, breakpoint scale), A171 item 2 (connection loaded after the first paint), A173 (own hooks), A179, A180.

## What exists

- `web/src/shell/`: `AppShell` (skip link, bar, one `main`), `NavBar` (Lobbies, Team Builder, Cost Tables, Tournaments, Calendar; current item marked; a scrolling row with pointer chevrons; Admin gear for staff), `AccountArea` (placeholder while not ready; "Sign in" for visitors and guests with a validated `next`; for members a chip with an initial and the name, opening a menu with Profile and Sign out), `ConnectionBanner` ("Reconnecting…" after 2 s), `RouteAnnouncer` (focus to the new page's heading and a polite announcement after a client-side navigation, not on first load, only after the viewer's first input).
- Full-screen splash pages stay outside the shell: standby, sign-in, 404, no-access, offline, maintenance, and the new load-failed splash.
- `LazyStdbProvider`: the app renders at once and the connection code loads in its own chunk after the first render; the status file is still read before any connection. `tokenStorage.ts` holds the connection-token helpers without the SDK, so sign-out needs no chunk.
- The `/` route loads the sign-in panel's chunk in its loader when `login=1`, with the panel's frame as the pending state and an error splash with Retry when the chunk cannot load.
- The auth snapshot carries the display name.

## Mockup comparison

Nine mockups have a bar; they agree on items, order and labels. Differences from the mockups, recorded in A180: staff-only Admin gear, "Sign in" for "LOG IN", an initial instead of an avatar, no footer, the tournaments item linked to its route, the active-lobby item left for the lobby room port, a `header` holding a `nav`, chevrons hidden from assistive technology. Breakpoint snap: the landing mockup's 768 px narrow rule is applied at the 560 step.

## Reviews

Two independent reviews (Fable 5.2), a fix pass after each (fresh Opus implementer for the fix passes).

1. Lazy connection and guards sound. Fixed: the first navigation to the sign-in panel moved no focus and announced the previous title; two tests passed only on a warm transform cache (reproduced failing 4 of 4 cold: a CI failure caught before the push); Escape from the account trigger; sign-out fetching a chunk that can vanish across a deploy; a visitor at 390 px seeing less than one item; chevrons taking focus; a blank sign-in address on a hard load; the pill over the standby chip.
2. Route loader and announcer held (no remount during a sign-in flow). Fixed: a partly visible item not scrolled into view on keyboard focus; the router's bare error screen when the sign-in chunk fails to load; announcer test gaps and its uncapped busy poll; two wrong numbers in the width table. The last pass was verified by the orchestrator's gate run including the cold-cache run; not reviewed a third time.

## Deviations and open points

- Deviations from the brief, accepted (A180 item 9): root layout instead of a pathless layout route; no small-screen menu (the mockups have none); the bar above an empty main while a guarded page waits.
- Open look question: the phone bar form (A180 item 3).
- Known limits (in `docs/frontend/pages.md`): two consecutive pages with the same title move focus but announce nothing; the pill and the chip are 5 px apart at 320 px; frosted bar contrast over bright content once pages scroll under it; the splash rule reads a private router field; no reconnecting notice on the sign-in panel; `spacetimedb/react` ships its own copy of the SDK codec (package build, not an import here).
- Not checked: Firefox, Safari, a real screen reader, touch input, a real slow network.
- Implementers: one tmp script edited with `sed -i`, two small scratch files left under `tmp/` and `.playwright-cli/`.
