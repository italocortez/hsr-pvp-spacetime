## Reducer signatures after S7b

- `set_eidolon(lobbyId, teamSide, characterName, eidolon: u8)` — a player seat of that team or any referee; Drafting (once drafted) → Scoring; open game; throttled (`lineupMinIntervalMs`); rejected while the draft is paused; under `requireOwnership` only a level the team owns on its selected accounts; Classic cost = the cost-set price at the level; Auction pays the extra delta (character budget first, then light cones, the committed bid reserved) or refunds it to the light-cone budget; logs `SetEidolon`; recomputes; resets both confirmations.
- `confirm_game(lobbyId, teamSide)` — the captain of that side, or a spectator referee with `refereeFullControl` (both sides at once); needs that side's lineup and complete scores; Auction pool gate; a side cannot confirm twice; the second confirmation closes the game (one `ConfirmGame` step, `closed: true`); a level-4 tie stays open with both flags set.
- `close_game(lobbyId, winnerOverride?, winnerOverrideReason?)` — a lobby referee, Moderator+, organizer or `canResolveResults` assistant; override only on a level-4 tie and required then; A39 seating at the close (tournament lobby: a seated referee without staff role cannot decide a tie; casual: accepted and audited).
- `nominate_character(lobbyId, characterName)` — E0 base cost; team ownership under `requireOwnership`; `baseCost ≤ charBudget` and `baseCost + extra ≤ charBudget + lcBudget`.
- `place_bid(lobbyId, bidAmount)` — unchanged signature; + team ownership, + `bid + extra ≤ charBudget + lcBudget`.
- `equip_lightcone`, `arrange_lineup` — Equipping or Scoring, open game, recompute + reset both confirmations; the S7 surcharge reservation is gone.
- `record_group_scores`, `set_score_adjustment` — unchanged signatures; open game (Admin path on a closed game); confirmations reset on the game row.
- `advance_stage` — Drafting → Equipping (D-50 rollover), Equipping → Scoring (signal); Scoring rejected (`Games close through confirm_game or close_game; the stage moves on the close.`).
- Removed: `confirm_lineup`, `confirm_match_scores`, `submit_match_result` (the helper `submitSeriesResult` runs at the deciding close).

## Orchestrator interpretations (beyond the decision rows; the user may overrule)

1. **Refunds go to the light-cone budget** in every stage. A61(6) names the pay path; a refund that restored character budget would let a team overdraw more than once, which A61 rules out; the budgets merge at the rollover anyway.
2. **`submit_match_result` removed**, not kept as an argument-less reducer: A60 rejected the referee click and no caller remains.
3. **A level-4 tie with both confirmations stays open** (flags set, no error) until a staff `close_game` with an override.
4. **Admin edits reach the current game only**: tie-breaks 2 and 3 read the game's steps, which `advance_to_next_game` deletes (S7 finding 5); after the deciding close the current game is the last one.
5. **`set_eidolon` callers**: any player seat of the team (like `equip_lightcone`) or any referee naming the team (like `arrange_lineup`).

## Implementer interpretations (Opus; accepted by the reviewer)

A side that already confirmed cannot confirm again; `set_eidolon` to the current level is rejected; one `ConfirmGame` step per close; an Admin edit that leaves a game tied keeps a prior staff override, only a tie without one is rejected; an Admin edit that decides a Pending series between games submits it; the Admin path skips the captain / referee checks and `equip_lightcone` takes the team from the character's owner; in Classic a `SetEidolon` step counts only when it follows the pick it edits (undone picks); the close / submit / admin-recompute helpers live in `helpers/gameClose.ts` (an import cycle through `runFinalization` kept them out of `scoringHelpers.ts`).
