# S10t — undo on every draft step, the confirm window before equipping, a stand-in rule (2026-11-10)

## Goal

Implement A127 and A132 (the referee's undo removes the last character step of any kind), A129 to A131 (the last pick or sale no longer starts equipping; a confirm window that is a lobby setting), A128 (a disqualified player cannot be a stand-in).

## Decisions implemented

- **A127, A132**: `undo_last_step` walks Ban, Pick, Nominate, Bid and AuctionSold, one per call, while the lobby is in Drafting. An eidolon selection is never undone by itself and never blocks an undo. Undoing a pick or a sale removes that character's eidolon selections. In Auction the sale's undo gives back, per budget, what the sale and those selections charged; costs of selections on other characters stay.
- **A129, A130, A131**: after the last pick or sale the lobby stays in Drafting for `confirmWindowSeconds` (lobby and preset setting, 0 to 300, default 60; 0 = no window). The referee may undo or call `advance_stage`; when the window has elapsed either client-called expiry reducer moves to Equipping with the D-50 roll-over.
- **A128**: `approve_stand_in` refuses a user whose own enrolment is Disqualified.
- Review rulings folded in: a concede or forfeit in the window counts like an Equipping concede for Ranked character stats (the behaviour before A129); the move to Equipping clears a pause.

## Review

Two independent Fable 5.2 reviews, read-only, each with its own in-memory probes (`notes/audit-2026-11-09/s10t-review.md`, `s10t-review-2.md`).

First review, **COMMIT**: 12 random Auction games (556 steps) and 5 Classic games (152 steps) undone against snapshots with 0 mismatches; 60 undo cycles bit-identical; the window refused at 59.999 s and accepted at 60.000 s; manual advance, expiry and window 0 leave identical state. Findings folded in: eidolon edits as undo steps could bury a pick inside a short window (resolved by A132); a concede in the window skipped Ranked character stats; `advance_stage` during a pause left the lobby paused for good.

Second review of the A132 fix pass, **COMMIT AFTER FIXES**:

- G1 (high): undoing a sale whose removed selections included a lowering could store a negative light-cone budget, and the next spend then created budget (probe: 34.5 total where the rules give 5). Fixed: a shortfall is taken from the character budget and 0 is stored; the tests assert both budgets stay at or above 0. The reviewer's sequence is a test with explicit values (6 / 0, then 5 / 0).
- G2 (medium): undoing a bid could restore a holder who can no longer pay, stalling the sale. Fixed with the one refusal inside the draft: "Cannot undo this bid: the previous bidder can no longer cover their bid." Reported to the user as a default.
- Held: 36 random Auction games with 2,498 undos (totals within 4.3e-5; 400 undos without an intervening edit bit-identical), mirror picks, re-picks, the owner's three-pick example.

The G1 and G2 fixes were verified by the orchestrator's suite run and by the reviewer's sequences turned into tests; they did not get a third independent review.

## Findings and hand-offs

- **Open with the user**: a rule that a team at 0 character budget may no longer pay eidolon costs from the light-cone budget (it would amend A61); the ten stand-in rulings of `notes/audit-2026-11-09/standin-audit.md`.
- The last Auction sale is always an A81 immediate sale (the other team is already full), so its undo removes the nomination and the sale together.
- The refund rule is "give back what that character cost" (option A, the orchestrator's recommendation; the user had not chosen): after "buy A, raise B with a spill into the light-cone budget, undo A" the split between the two budgets differs from a world where A was never bought; the total is the same.
- A one-unit residue in the last place of an f32 budget can remain when a selection on another character lies between a sale and its undo.
- The window is as soft as a turn timer: if no client calls the expiry, nothing moves; a player's manual pause holds the window.
- Either expiry reducer closes the window whatever the draft mode; no abuse path found.
- With `confirmWindowSeconds` 0 the move also posts the "Draft complete" chat line, which the old automatic move did not.
- The client must take "draft complete" from the session, not from the stage; the room page needs a countdown, a "go to equipping" control for the referee and a live undo control (M-steps).
- A referee who is also a seated captain can take back their own nomination before a bid.
- Deviations from a decision row: none.
