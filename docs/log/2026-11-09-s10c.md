## Decisions implemented

- **A81** (user, 2026-11-09): Auction, a full team sits out. It neither nominates nor bids; the other team nominates and buys at base cost with no bidding round.
- **A82** (user): Auction character stats follow the sale's winning team; every player of that team gets the played stat.
- **A83** (user): the concede summary is returned by the member view only to a Moderator or Admin.
- **A84** (user): a re-resolve, rollback or override is refused while the downstream bracket match has a lobby or a live record.
- **A85** (user): the reconnect lifts an auto-pause only on the away player's return with no seated player still away; `resume_draft` on an auto-pause is open to the referee and to present seated players of both teams.
- Without a decision row (the contract already said so): eidolon bound in `pick_character`, superimposition bound in `equip_lightcone`, finite non-negative bids in `place_bid`, the two misspelt synergy names in the seed and a character-exists check on synergy writes.
