# S10k — played stats follow ownership (2026-11-10)

## Goal

Implement A110: the played character stat goes to the players of the owning team who own the character, whoever made the pick. A111 (cursor events stay public, row filter tested at S11) and A112 (a guest's tournament entry moves at sign-in, S10l) were recorded during this step.

## Decisions implemented

- **A110**: for a manual Classic pick, a Classic timer auto-pick and an Auction sale alike, each non-coach player of the owning team who owns the character on any of their game accounts gets the played stat; no recorded owner and one player: that player; no recorded owner and two or more players: nobody. Faced and global rows unchanged.

## Findings and hand-offs

- **Open for the user (review N1): tournament games read ownership when the tournament is finalized, not when the game was played.** Tournament records are finalized by the tournament's finalize job at Completed. A player who adds a character to their roster between the game and the end of the tournament gets the played stat for a game where nobody on the team owned it; removing one loses the credit. This satisfies A110(5) as written ("read when the match is finalized"); freezing ownership at the game's close would need the credited players stored on the game.
- **A deleted participant on a team of two or more** (review N2): the deleted player's accounts are gone but they still count as a candidate, so a character only they owned credits nobody and the remaining teammate is not treated as a lone player. Chosen reading of A110(3).
- **Indirect signal** (review N5): achievements read the played stats and are public, so a character-specific "played" achievement now implies ownership or solo play. It does not identify a seat. Captains who do not own their team's characters earn fewer of these than before.
- Untested parts of A110 worth a later T-task (review N3): the tournament deferred path, an abandoned or deleted participant, an account with `isActive = false`.
- Deviations from a decision row: none.
