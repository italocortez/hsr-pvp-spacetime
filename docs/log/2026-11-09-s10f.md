## The turn clock

`budgetMs = accumulatedPauseMs > 0 ? accumulatedPauseMs : standardTurnSeconds * 1000`; `remainingMs = isPaused ? budgetMs : max(0, budgetMs - (now - turnStartAt))`. A pause stores the remaining time (at least 1 ms) as the budget and restarts `turnStartAt`; a resume keeps the budget and restarts `turnStartAt`; a new turn writes budget 0. Used by `pause_draft`, the auto-pause, `resume_draft`, `liftAutoPauseOnReturn` and both expiry reducers. The views pass `timerState` through unchanged, so a client computes the same value.

## Limits chosen in this step

300 edit steps per edited team side and 300 confirmation steps per confirming side; 10 s between manual pauses of the same party (Blue, Red, referee); 30 result entries per user per 10 s window; 20 adjustments per record, team sum within ±100000000 hundredths; event title 100, description 1000, account label 32; cost fields 0 to 100000, synergy modifier within ±100000.

## Review

Independent Fable 5.2 reviewer, read-only, verdict **COMMIT AFTER FIXES** (`scratchpad/s10f-review.md`). The turn clock was traced through every writer and reader and found correct: a turn cannot gain time beyond rounding and the 1 ms floor, expire early, or fail to expire; there are no scheduled timer rows. Folded in by a fix pass:

- F1 (gating): the edit-step cap was one counter per lobby, so one captain could exhaust it and block the other team's edits and both confirmations; now per team side.
- F2: the adjustment sum bound could be bypassed by a delete or a move naming the other team; now checked on the team the entry belongs to.
- F3: the pause interval was lobby-wide; now per pausing party.

## Findings and hand-offs

- **Rework before the review:** the first version of results 7 reused the 250 ms lineup stamp for score entry, which rejected a referee saving both teams' scores at once and broke 162 tests; replaced by the 30-per-10-s window.
- **Standalone override is stricter than the audit suggested** (review F4): Pending and Rejected records are refused; a parked Pending record is resolved with `admin_force_finalize` / `admin_void_match`.
- **Classic timer auto-pick stats** (review F5): as committed here, no player gets the played stat. The user ruled the whole team gets it (A106, S10h).
- **A pause step's party is resolved from the actor's current member row**: a referee transfer inside the 10 s re-labels that actor's earlier pause. Accepted edge case.
- **Results 10** (history views return every match the caller ever played) is not fixed: the bound depends on how the history page loads data.
- The seed change cannot be unit-tested without a connection (`it.todo`).
