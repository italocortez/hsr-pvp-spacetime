## Findings and hand-offs

- **Membership throttle is a fixed window** (20 per 10 s), not a minimum interval: a strict interval rejects normal back-to-back actions (join, then take a seat). A burst of up to 40 around a window edge is possible.
- `transfer_host` and `confirm_ready` / `unconfirm_ready` / `shuffle_teams` are unthrottled (review N4): for the throttle pass.
- `uniqueUsername` does not avoid `Guest_<hex8>` names (review N2; same older gap in `update_username`).
- The two rate-state columns need the S11 clear-publish; a non-clearing publish before it would fail.
- LOW findings left, with reason: lobby 13, 15, 16 (need a decision), 17, 21, 25 (tied to pending rulings), 14 rest, 19, 20, 22, 23, 26; identity 10 to 13, 15 to 18. The ban-on-reconnect branch in `index.ts` was kept as a guard for restored or hand-edited rows. Unused indexes, `LinkRequest`, `PlayerSnapshot` and other dead symbols stay for the clean-up step.
