# S10u — Auction undo goes back to the nomination; no light-cone dip at 0 character budget (2026-11-10)

## Goal

Implement A134 (in the auction phase an undo removes the whole nomination round) and A133 (a team at 0 character budget cannot pay eidolon costs from the light-cone budget during the draft). The step also repairs two defects of the S10t commit found by a third review of it.

## Why this step exists

The user asked for an independent review of the last two S10t fixes (`notes/audit-2026-11-09/s10t-review-3.md`). It found, in the committed code:

- F1 (high): undoing a sale reopened the bid with the winner holding it; by f32 rounding the refunded budget could be below the bid, and with the bid-undo refusal the draft deadlocked (one random game reached it unaided).
- F2 (high): under ownership the bid-undo check ignored the default eidolon extra, and a clamp then created budget (3 in the probe).
- F4: a team could make the bid undo impossible for the rest of the draft by spending after being outbid.
- F3 (low): a light-cone budget of minus one unit in the last place.

All four came from one design choice: an undo restored a team as holder of a bid. The user's rule A134 removes that choice.

## Decisions implemented

- **A134**: with a round open, an undo deletes the nomination and all its bids; no budget moves. After a sale, an undo deletes the sale with its nomination, bids and the winner's eidolon selections on that character (A132) and gives back per budget what they charged. Afterwards nothing is open, nobody holds a bid, the same team nominates again on a fresh clock. Bans and Classic picks stay one step per call. The bid-undo refusal and `BidPayload.previousAmount` are removed.
- **A133**: in Auction while the lobby is in Drafting (the confirm window included), an eidolon raise may use the light-cone budget only when the team does not hold the bid and has character budget left; that charge empties the character budget. A holder's raise must fit its free character budget. A team with no character budget cannot nominate or bid on a character with a cost or a default extra above 0; `EMPTY` stays legal. A sale is one charge. Lowering refunds to the light-cone budget. In Equipping and later the pool rule is unchanged.

## Review

Two independent Fable 5.2 reviews of this step, read-only, each with its own probes (`notes/audit-2026-11-09/s10u-review.md`, `s10u-review-2.md`).

First review, **COMMIT AFTER FIXES**. A134 held: 96 seeded random games, 75,334 actions, no negative budget, totals from prices exact, no stuck state, every game finished; an auction always finishes with depleted teams (`EMPTY` is always legal). On A133:

- F1: a team could dip several times by raising while it held a bid. Fixed: no spill while holding.
- F2: a sale whose extra grew after the bid guard (admin edit, deletion of a seated user) was refused and could stall. Fixed: the sale applies the highest affordable level, down to E0.
- F3: a character budget with a rounding residue counted as above 0. Fixed with one named tolerance, 2^-20 of the starting character budget.
- F6 (pre-existing): the minimum-raise check compared f32 values in double precision. Fixed.

Second review of the fix pass, **COMMIT AFTER FIXES** (text only): 96 games, 71,715 actions, 0 failures; 181 dips counted, never more than one per team per stretch without an undo that returns character budget; 747 holder raises, none touched the light-cone budget. Folded in: two false doc statements; G3 (the zero test refuses only a charge that needs the light-cone budget); G4 (with a minimum raise above 0 a bid must be strictly above the current bid).

The last small pass (G3, G4, texts) was verified by the orchestrator's suite run; it implements the reviewer's stated fixes with the reviewer's cases as tests.

## Findings and hand-offs

- **Open with the user (review 2, G2)**: after a step-down sale under ownership a team can sit at an eidolon level it does not own and cannot leave, and is scored at that level. Only outside events cause it (admin roster or cost edits, deletion of a seated user, an abandoned member's roster). Options put to the user: keep; owned levels only and cancel the round when none is affordable (recommended); owned levels only, else E0.
- "One dip per team" holds per stretch of the draft without an undo that returns character budget: an undone sale gives budget back and the team can dip again.
- After the dip a lowering is one-way until Equipping.
- A holder whose whole character budget is reserved cannot raise at all.
- A cost row deleted outright during a draft still makes pass, timer and bid fail ("has no cost", A107); the referee's undo of the round gets out of it.
- The tolerance scales with the starting budget (about 9.5e-5 at 100, 0.095 at the 100000 cap).
- Not probed by the reviewers: the Thirds format, away members and disconnect auto-pause during a round, tournament lobbies.
- Deviations from a decision row: none.
