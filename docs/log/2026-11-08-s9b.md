## Views as implemented

- **Identity.** `view_my_profile`: per-sender, at most one row, PK `id`: `{ username, displayName, avatarCharacterName, title?, hasDiscordLinked, isGuest, role, loginMethod?, providerId?, providerUsername? }`; no presence, e-mail, Discord, `deletedAt` or audit columns. `view_my_identity`: `{ identity, userId }`. `view_user_by_username(username)` (anonymous arg view): exact match through the unique `username` index; a soft-deleted user → `[]`; public columns only. `view_user_achievements(userId)` (anonymous arg view): `{ achievementId, awardedAt }`, no awarder (A43 / A40). `view_admin_user_private`: Moderator+, PK `userId`, provider fields, no audit columns.
- **Stats.** `view_my_mmr_ratings`: per-sender `{ gameMode, seasonId, rating, matchesPlayed, wins, lastUpdatedAt }` (`wins` from the season + mode `PlayerStat`); `MmrRating` stays private. `view_my_player_stats` / `view_my_character_stats`: no `userId`, no audit ids (their writers stamp the finalizing user). `Leaderboard` (public table, no view): `rebuildLeaderboard(ctx, seasonId?)` keeps the top 20 per category ordered by rating, then wins, then user id (total order), audit actor `SYSTEM_USER_ID`; `incrementGlobalCharacterStat` and the auto-award in `checkAndAwardAchievements(ctx, userId)` stamp SYSTEM (`awardedById` too); a manual award keeps the staff id; no other writer of those three tables exists.
- **Roster and social.** `view_public_hsr_accounts` (anonymous): public rosters through the new `HsrAccount.is_roster_public` index; the owner identified (a public roster is the owner's choice; S9b-A2 states the residual risk). `view_my_roster_visibility`: follows A40 (not the D-18 override), + `joinSeq`; `memberUserId` / `hsrAccountId` 0 when hidden; any referee sees every roster (D-14) with ids masked unless the referee is a spectator (D-71) — the seated-referee rating exposure is an open ruling (below). `view_my_relationships`: + the other player's `displayName`, avatar, title and `otherMmrByMode` (A40 CH-M1); no audit ids. `view_my_roster` unchanged.
- **Calendar** (`views/calendarViews.ts`, per-sender, index-only, labels through `resolveUserLabel`, no audit columns): `view_my_calendar_events` (own, invited, and events organized by the players whose calendars the caller saved, `isVisible` filtered server-side; a tournament-linked event visible to both teams' members), `view_my_calendar_invites` (PK `inviteKey`; the caller's invites plus every invite on the caller's own events, so the organizer sees the RSVP list), `view_my_availability`, `view_saved_calendar_availability`, `view_my_saved_calendars`.
- **Admin** (`views/adminViews.ts`, Moderator+ else `[]`; whole-table reads after the role check, the documented exemption to the no-scan rule): `view_admin_user_identities` (no `kind`: the table has none), `view_admin_ban_records`, `view_admin_deleted_users`, `view_admin_presence` (+ `lastLoginAt`), `view_admin_lobby_bans`, `view_admin_abuse_config` (a Moderator reads it; only an Admin changes it). Not built: `view_admin_lobbies`, `view_admin_match_results`, `view_admin_match_history` (PAGE-MAP does not list them).

## Findings and deviations

1. **Open ruling: seated referee and `ClosedNoRating`** (review #1): D-14 lets any referee see every roster; S9a withholds the exact rating from a seated referee; recommendation: narrow D-14 to the spectator referee.
2. **Admin views read whole tables** (review #2, #3; T-S9b): the honest `.iter()` after the Moderator+ check replaces an index-range walk of the same cost; the rule is now "no `.iter()` in any view a non-staff client can subscribe"; the gate exempts `adminViews.ts` and the `view_admin_user_private` block.
3. **Public-roster residual risk** (S9b-A2): a public roster identifies its owner by the owner's choice; stated in `docs/anonymous-play/contract.md` as an extension of A40's accepted risk (worth one read by the user).
4. **Catalogue**: three Appendix B admin views not built (PAGE-MAP does not list them); four views beyond Appendix B exist (`view_admin_stuck_games`, `view_my_match_lineup_history`, `view_my_mmr_ratings`, `view_user_by_username`), all from decision rows or page-map rules.
5. **Egress backlog** (review #7): relationship and calendar rows depend on more tables than before; revisit in the egress pass with the dev meter.
6. **Usage numbers** are the harness task-notification figures.
