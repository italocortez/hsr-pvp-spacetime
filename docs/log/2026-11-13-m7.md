## Behaviour

- The app reads `/status.json` (`no-store`, 3 s timeout) before it builds a connection. `maintenance: true` (exact boolean): no connection, maintenance splash. Anything else, including every read failure: connect as before.
- Retry reads the file at once, on both splashes.
- Offline tabs re-read at once or within 30 s, then every 60 s. Reconnecting tabs read 30 s after the drop, then every 60 s; a short blip reads nothing. Hidden tabs wait until visible. Never two reads within 30 s without a click. A `true` answer shows the maintenance splash and removes the provider, which ends the SDK's rebuild and reconnect loops.
- A tab on the maintenance splash recovers by Retry or reload only.
- `?maintenance=skip` sets a per-tab `sessionStorage` flag that ignores the file. The module still refuses non-Admins (B-8).
- One permanent `ConnectionScope` wraps the app; the status answer never remounts it. Only `StdbProvider.tsx` imports `spacetimedb/react`, and the SDK's own React hooks have no provider above a page.
- CSP: `script-src 'self' 'unsafe-eval'` is required (the SDK builds its codecs with `Function()`; without it the page stays empty: proven in the browser). `connect-src` holds the two maincloud origins and `https://*.ufs.sh`. `style-src 'unsafe-inline'` kept from the plan; `'self'` alone already works (proposal).
- Rewrite: unknown paths go to `/index.html`, except `/api/`, `/assets/`, the named root files, any path whose last segment has an extension, and dot-segments. A future route with a dot in its last segment would 404.
- The `functions` block is left out until `api/` exists (M5).

Three independent reviews (Fable 5.2), each followed by a fix pass (Opus).

1. No blocking defect. Fixed: app remount on every load, tabs that failed before the file flipped never re-read, a wrong `runtime` note for M5, HTML answers for missing files, ignore list gaps, gate holes, four test gaps. The maintenance order in the doc was reversed (file first, then module).
