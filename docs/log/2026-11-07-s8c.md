## Rules as implemented

- **Generation**: withdraw still-Forming teams (A26a) → ≥ 2 Ready teams with unique seeds → format validations (A29 third place, `groupSize ≥ 2`, `groupAdvanceCount` 1..2, ≥ 2 qualifiers) → manual groups read before `deleteBracket` (which clears `groupId`) → delete an existing bracket with its stand-ins and calendar rows (T20) → elimination rows over `seedNumber` order, or group rows (manual groups validated: every Ready team placed, exact group count, every group ≥ `groupSize` when there are two or more groups; one group holding every team is accepted when the Ready count is below `groupSize`, like the auto split) → `bracketGeneratedAt` stamped. GroupInto* formats get their elimination rows from `advance_group_stage` (status-gated qualifiers; elimination rows detected by `bracketSide`, because the grand final key also starts with G); non-qualifiers become `Eliminated`. Every plain `Error` path of the pure module is pre-empted by a `SenderError`.
- **Staff**: `resolve_bracket_match` applies `Staff` on a Ready match (its calendar event deleted first, D-22), rolls back and re-applies on a Decided one, same winner no-op; `rollback_bracket_match` refuses while anything downstream is Decided and deletes the calendar event; reasons trimmed, ≤ 1000, logged (no column); both leave `// S8d` markers for the record / lobby side effects (D.8.3); the old MMR guard is gone (A24).
- **DQ**: loop until the disqualified team holds no open slot: a Ready match → `applyBracketResult(opponent, 'Dq', 0-0)` and its calendar event deleted; a Pending match → the team's slot becomes a BYE (`bracketStore.setSlotBye`) and `settle` walks the opponent over once it arrives; in double elimination the DQ loss drops the team into the losers bracket where the same rule applies. Known limit (documented): once a slot is a BYE, its feeder match cannot be rolled back (the helper's message is the raw slot message; a clearer text is a polish item). An emptied team becomes Disqualified (not deleted); a DQ in Seeding after generation stamps `lastSeedChangeAt` when the team empties; a team left non-empty after generation **keeps its status** (A26a(3): it plays with stand-ins); before generation it follows `refreshTeamStatus`.
- Legacy seed modes kept (`'mmr'` ignores the season, A36 has no season logic yet).
