## B. Findings, ranked by user impact

**1. Shelved Leave is a one-way door (dead end).** User: presses Leave in a shelved series (or any member of a shelved lobby). Backend: normal path deletes the row (L:461 lists only the four active stages; L:548); the lobby survives at count 0 (L:561-569); `join_lobby` accepts a new join only in Waiting / Drafting (L:325-327) and the reconnect branch needs a row (L:261). Expected: "Players may leave and return later" (S:142), LC:158/165 "preserved for return", LC:1108, D-19, the user's stated intent. Also `resume_series` reads `voluntarilyLeft` on rows that no longer exist (S:181-189), so "present" silently means "never pressed Leave". Classification: **architecture bug** against D-19's stated purpose, plus a **design gap** (no contract line says what Leave does in Shelved).

**2. A host who leaves in Waiting or Shelved leaves a headless room (dead end).** User: the host presses Leave before the draft (or while shelved). Backend: `transferHost` / `transferReferee` run only in the active path (L:489-492); the normal path deletes the row and keeps `hostUserId` (L:548, 572-575). Consequences: nobody can `start_draft` (`draftClassic.ts:38-42`), change settings, kick, close, shelve or resume (`ensureHostOrAbove` H:82-88; `hasSeriesAuthority` S:20-21). Waiting: everyone must leave so the room auto-closes, or wait 30 min for GC. Shelved: dead until GC (casual) or staff (tournament). The referee flag leaves with the host too, and `reclaim_referee` is a no-op when nobody holds the flag (RM:87-89), so a referee-less lobby stays referee-less. Expected: D-36 "host transfers to the next eligible member" (LA:296-299) reads as a lobby rule, not an active-stage rule; plain sense. Classification: **architecture bug**.

**3. Kick has no stage and no consequences (dead end).** User: host or `refereeCanKick` referee kicks a player mid-draft or mid-scoring (e.g. an AFK teammate, or the whole opposing team). Backend: no stage guard (L:629-697); the row is deleted without `voluntarilyLeft`, transfers or auto-concede (contrast L:483-516); LMA rows survive (LA:184). The kicked player can never return (L:325). An emptied team cannot be forfeited (`isForfeitEligible` returns false for zero players, D:102), cannot confirm a game (captain-only, `matchResultSubmission.ts:76-77`), and a player-referee cannot concede for the other side (C:131-141), so the match can only end by the kicker's own concede or by GC after everyone goes offline (void, no result). The kicked captain's `MatchResultParticipant` row still earns the credit (copy rule). Expected: LC:217-255 describes kick as a lobby-room action ("was kicked from the lobby"); D-21 / D-32 say who, never when. Classification: **design gap** (stage × slot never decided) with an architecture consequence.

**4. D-22 treats a shelved series as "in a lobby" (inconsistent).** User: a member of a shelved series tries to join or create another lobby, or delete their account. Backend: `ensureNotInLobby` skips AwaitingResult and `voluntarilyLeft` only (H:37); the shelf lasts up to 72 h casual (G:129) or indefinitely for tournaments (G:125); `delete_my_account` uses the same test (`reducers/profile.ts:50-57`). The rejection text tells them to leave (H:40), which is finding 1. Spectators, coaches and staff members of a shelved tournament lobby cannot create or join the next round's lobby (TL:76; A34 makes staff ordinary members). Expected: D-22's purpose is one *active* lobby; A24(2) already exempted the parked stage for the same reason. Classification: **design gap**.

**5. BetweenGames is a trap and contradicts Shelved from the user's chair (inconsistent / dead end).** Same moment ("between games"), opposite rules: Leave in BetweenGames is permanent and may concede the series (L:461, 500-516); Leave twenty seconds later, after the host pressed Shelve, deletes the row and keeps the series. Worse: a team that vanishes in BetweenGames gets no `disconnectedAt` (I:17, 269: the S4 review fix #1 excluded BetweenGames together with Shelved, `docs/log/2026-11-06-s4.md:79`), so `claim_forfeit` can never succeed against it (D:108); the waiting team cannot leave without conceding its own series (L:505-513), cannot shelve unless its host is online, and GC only fires once the waiting team itself goes offline for 30 min (G:141-144, 157-160), which voids the match. A shelved series cannot be conceded either (C:100-103): the only exits are resume (both teams present) or GC. Expected: D-10 "BetweenGames is active" was about leave semantics, not about removing the forfeit clock; A22a / D-08 intend the clock in every stage where a disconnect matters. Classification: **design gap** (interaction of D-10, S4 finding 1 and D-15 never specified).

**6. Closing the tab is always better than pressing Leave (smell).** In every stage past Waiting the tab close keeps the row, moves flags only after 15 s and allows return; the button is permanent (live stages, BetweenGames) or deletes the player (Shelved). The only mockup copy for Leave is Waiting copy: "You'll return to the lobby browser and your seat opens up for someone else." (`design/mockups/lobbyroom.html:2293-2305`); in Drafting the seat does not open, you cannot return and your team may forfeit. Expected: a destructive control states its consequence. Classification: **UI gap** (per-stage copy) rooted in the design gap of findings 1 and 5.

**7. ## C. Recommended model

One sentence a user can carry: **a player's seat belongs to them from the first draft to the result; "Leave" frees a seat only where a seat can be refilled (Waiting) or no longer matters (after the result); mid-series you either forfeit or you step away and come back.**

Flag rule, every stage: **a lobby with members always has a host and a referee.** Any removal of a row (leave, kick, ban, DQ) transfers both flags if the leaver held them, candidates in the A22(5) order (host → Blue → Red → spectators → coaches by `joinSeq`), online first, then any remaining member; `reclaim_referee` gives the flag to the caller when nobody holds it. Flags never return automatically to the original holder (today's rule); the new holder can hand the referee flag back with `transfer_referee`.

Resume rule: `resume_series` requires at least one **online** non-coach, non-left player per team (`isOnline && !voluntarilyLeft`, with the A48 presence test), clears every `disconnectedAt`, and re-points `hostUserId` to an online player when the host is absent. Callable by the series authority, and, when no authority holder is online, by any online player of either team (both teams are present by the gate, so this is mutual consent).

### C.5 Crediting

Under the copy rule (S8d as implemented, A66 as recommended) `MatchResultParticipant` rows are written once at `start_draft` (`draftClassic.ts:162-181`) and nothing in this model changes who gets a result: leaving, shelving, kicking and stepping away only decide who can still *play* (captain, confirm, forfeit clock). Under the A54 live-roster reading the model is still safe for every stage before the result, because it never deletes a player's row between `start_draft` and AwaitingResult (kick → forced leave keeps the row; Leave is refused in BetweenGames / Shelved); the one point that would differ is Leave in AwaitingResult, which under the live reading would have to be refused for players until finalization. The D-75 fallback (`finalizationHelpers.ts:275-283`) becomes unreachable under the copy rule and can go with A66.
