## Behaviour

- Pages not ported yet render the standby page with their name.
- `_authed`: a visitor is sent to `/?login=1&next=<path>`. `_admin`: the same, and a user below Moderator is sent to `/`. Guards are a convenience; the module stays the authority.
- `next` is kept only if it is a string of at most 2048 characters that starts with a single `/` and holds no control character and no backslash.
- A change of the auth snapshot re-runs the guards.
- A dev-only `window.__devAuth(...)` switches the pretended role; it is absent from the production bundle.
