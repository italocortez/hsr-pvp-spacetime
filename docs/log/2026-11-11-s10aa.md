# S10aa — reserve time, late actions, manual lift of the automatic pause (2026-11-11)

## Goal

Act on the timer review of 2026-11-10 (`notes/audit-2026-11-09/timers-review.md`): make the reserve bank work, close late actions, stop the automatic resume on return.

## Decisions implemented

- **A148**: after the turn time the on-clock team's reserve drains and the turn stays with it; only overtime is charged; at turn 0 and reserve 0 the expiry auto-plays. Classic and Auction, including the team that must answer a bid.
- **A149**: an undo restores both reserves to their values at the start of the turn returned to; the turn clock restarts full.
- **A150**: pick, ban, nominate, bid and pass are refused more than 1000 ms after all time is gone ("Time is up for this turn.").
- **A151**: no return lifts an automatic pause; it is resumed by hand.
- **A153**: an automatic pause is resumed by a present seated player of the paused team or by a referee; the opponent is refused.
- **A152**, **A154**: recorded, no code change (manual pause has no limit; a pause after all time is gone stays allowed; pinned by a test).

## Clock model

- `teamBlueReserveMs` / `teamRedReserveMs`: each team's reserve at the start of the current turn.
- `accumulatedPauseMs`: turn time left at `turnStartAt` (0 = full turn); `inReserve`: the turn time was already spent before the anchor; `reserveUsedMs`: reserve used in this turn before the anchor.
- `reserveMarks`: for each turn-ending step that charged reserve, the reserves at that turn's start; an undo reads the earliest mark at or after the step returned to and drops later ones.
- One function, `readClock` in `helpers/draft/turnClock.ts`, serves every reducer; the same formula for clients is in `docs/match-session/architecture.md`. No view changed: the inputs already arrive through `view_my_match_session` and `view_my_lobby`.
- The cause of an automatic pause is the `actorSlot` (team) of its `Pause` step, written by the 15 s check.

## Review

Independent Fable 5.2 reviewer, read-only, own model written from the decision rows, verdict **COMMIT AFTER FIXES** (`notes/audit-2026-11-09/s10aa-review.md`).

- Random walks: 331,200 actions in 1,936 lobbies (Classic and Auction, bans None and Four, reserve 0, 7 and 120 s, random pick and EMPTY, window 0 and 20 s), 0 mismatches. No drift, refill, negative reserve, wrong team charged, stale mark, early expiry or late acceptance.
- F1: a pause on a spent turn reopens it for about 1 s. The user kept it (A154).
- F2: an opponent could resume an automatic pause while the dropped player was away, draining that team's turn and reserve. Fixed (A153).
- F3: `reserveMarks` adds 12 bytes per overtime turn to the session row (largest seen 6, forced 16). Left.
- F4: reserve above 0 was tested in one file only. Tests added: the system preset's 120 s, a tournament lobby, a stand-in drop; the walk now covers reserve 0 and 120 s, random pick, and a dropped player who stays away.
- F5: whole-millisecond rounding (about 2 ms over 8 overtime turns). Left.

The fix pass (A153, the A154 pin, the F4 tests) was verified by the orchestrator's suite run and not independently reviewed.

## Findings and hand-offs

- After a resume while the dropped player is still away, no second automatic pause is armed; that team's turns expire one by one. With A153 only that team or a referee can choose this.
- When no player of the paused team is present (a 1v1 drop), only a referee resumes; the 15 s check moves the referee flag to an online member when the referee drops.
- A team may pause on its own turn (3 per team) and a manual pause has no limit; discussed with the user, kept.
- Frontend (room page): both countdowns from the documented formula with a server-time offset; turn clock only in Drafting; an expiry refused while paused must be sent again after the resume; "Time is up for this turn." handled; a resume control for the paused team and referee.
- Not probed by the reviewer: tournament lobbies, Thirds, 3v3, anonymous lobbies in a run.
- Deviations from a decision row: none.
