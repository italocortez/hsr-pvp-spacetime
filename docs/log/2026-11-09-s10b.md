# S10b — package-edit callers, calendar caps, referee stage powers (2026-11-09)

## Goal

Close the two items A76(4) left open and one question raised while doing so, before the frontend is built on the module: who may edit a game package, how the calendar event cap counts, and who starts a draft and advances its stages. Reducer logic only: no schema change, no publish.

## Decisions implemented

- **A78** (user, 2026-11-09): `set_eidolon`, `equip_lightcone`, `unequip_lightcone` and `arrange_lineup` accept the captain of the named team or any lobby referee; the two light-cone reducers take `teamSide` and use the lineup throttle. Fixes the Admin closed-game path, which could not reach Red's copy of a mirror-picked character.
- **A79** (user, 2026-11-09): the event cap counts 40 not-yet-ended events; a ceiling of 100 retained events per organizer; `create_calendar_event` rejects an end time in the past and `update_calendar_event` rejects a changed end time in the past. The 90-day cleanup is unchanged.
- **A80** (user, 2026-11-09): `start_draft` and `advance_stage` belong to the member holding the referee flag, or a Moderator / Admin; the host as such no longer qualifies.
- **A77** (user, 2026-11-09) is recorded but not implemented here: `User` private and `UserPrivate` merged into it land with the S11 clear-publish.

## Findings and hand-offs

- **Light-cone swap** needs an unequip and an equip at least `lineupMinIntervalMs` (250 ms) apart: the lobby room page must serialize the two calls.
- **Active cap through update** (review F2): an organizer can move ended events back into the future through `update_calendar_event`, which has no cap check, up to the 100 ceiling. Inside the letter of A79; open for the user.
- **100 ceiling** blocks an organizer with more than 100 bracket-match events in 90 days until manual deletes. Decided behaviour, noted.
- **Away referee:** `ensureRefereeOrAbove` checks the flag only; a referee who left but still holds the flag can advance until the A22a / A68 transfer moves it.
- Check order: the caller rule runs before the stage check, so a non-member gets the caller text.
- Stale comments naming only `arrange_lineup` for the lineup throttle (`abuseConfig.ts`, `lobbyMember.ts`, `abuseControls.ts`, `disconnectHelpers.ts`) and the unused `slotToTeamSide` import in `postDraft.ts`: left for the audit clean-up.
- Deviations from a decision row: none.
