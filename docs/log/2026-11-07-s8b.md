## Orchestrator decisions during the step (the user may overrule)

1. **A26a over the stricter reading of A26 for Forming teams.** The brief (review §5.1) withdrew every ineligible team at registration close; A26a's Definitions keep staff able to promote, add or move players into short teams until the bracket is generated and withdraw the still-Forming teams at that point. Implemented: at the entry into Seeding, eligible teams become Ready and the rest stay Forming; only the teams that did not check in are withdrawn (CheckIn path); S8c withdraws the still-Forming teams at generation (marker left in the legacy `generate_bracket`).
2. **`add_team_member(teamId, userId)`** added for A26a's "move an enrolled player into a short team" (`participants`, until `bracketGeneratedAt`).
3. **Idle cancel scope** kept at Registration through InProgress, wider than A24(6)'s "in-progress" wording; the idle measure for InProgress tournaments includes the newest `BracketMatch.lastModifiedDate` (review #4), so an active tournament is never cancelled.

## Implementer interpretations (Opus; reviewed)

Explicit guest rejection with a tournament message instead of `ensureVerifiedUser`; random-team opt-in through the new register argument; `hsrAccountIds` omitted = all accounts, a promoted waitlisted player gets all accounts locked; a team is checked in when any member is CheckedIn; team creation / joining needs approval when the tournament requires it; leaving / disbanding rejected in solo tournaments, solo teams not renamable; `add_participant` enforces capacity and verification but skips the minimum-MMR and roster checks; `groupSize ≥ 2` for group formats only (was ≥ 3 for every format); a manual group change stamps `lastSeedChangeAt`; `minimumMmr` without an EloConfig row throws the finalization error; assistant duplicates upserted, self-assignment rejected, a Moderator may list the organizer.

## Transitional limit

The legacy `generate_bracket` sets neither `bracketGeneratedAt` nor `groupId`, so Seeding → InProgress cannot pass `canStartInProgress` and the promote / add window does not close until S8c lands. T-S8b reaches InProgress by stamping the rows directly (`generateAndStamp` in `s8-fixtures.ts`); one test pins the unstamped behaviour.

## Review

Independent **Fable** reviewer over the raw diff, in parallel with T-S8b. Verified the permission helper and every swapped call against §5.4, the stage machine and its validations, the registration rules (capacity, DQ, waitlist, approval, minimum MMR with the installed SDK's `ctx.random.shuffle` and the EloConfig precedent, locks, windows), the team rules, create / update, determinism, bindings and docs. Verdict **COMMIT AFTER FIXES** (#1, #2 gating); all six items were applied by the implementer and tests were added for #1, #2, #4, #5, #6 before the commit.

| # | Severity | Finding | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Medium | `closeRegistration` ran only on entering Seeding; A26a defines "registration closes" as the transition out of Registration, so with check-in on, random teams were formed after check-in from players who could never check in and bypassed it. | **Fixed**: Registration → CheckIn runs the close effects (requests deleted, random teams formed, status refreshed); the entry into Seeding runs `enterSeeding` (unchecked teams Withdrawn on the CheckIn path, Ready pass, ≥ 2 eligible). |
| 2 | Medium | Anonymous solo tournaments named the team after `displayName` when the player set no alias (and for every `add_participant` target), exposing the identity through the public team list (A36a, A37). | **Fixed**: neutral `Player <n>` until `set_anonymous_alias` renames the team; non-anonymous solo keeps the display name. |
| 3 | Low | The Forming-team reading (A26a over A26 / §5.1) is a row-vs-row call; a Phase History row contradicted the code. | Row reworded; **the reading is put to the user** (decision 1 above). |
| 4 | Low | Idle = `Tournament.lastModifiedDate` only, so an InProgress tournament playing matches looked idle; the stage set is wider than A24(6). | **Fixed** (match activity counts); **the stage set is put to the user** (decision 3 above). |
| 5 | Low | In CheckIn the last member's self-withdrawal deleted the team (TD-18: never delete a team after registration closes). | **Fixed**: Withdrawn from CheckIn on, deleted only in Registration. |
| 6 | Low | Auto-generated solo team names skipped the uniqueness rule. | **Fixed**: numeric suffix on collision at registration, `add_participant` and alias changes. |

## Findings and deviations

1. The §5.1 table of the intent review predates A26a; where they differed (Forming teams at close), the decision row won and the brief was corrected mid-step.
2. The "registration closes" definition matters with check-in on: the close effects (requests, random teams) must run when leaving Registration, the eligibility pass when entering Seeding. The reviewer caught the gap; the implementer split `closeRegistration` / `enterSeeding`.
3. 29 more `// S10: rewrite` markers in the live suites (A36 arguments with neutral defaults; the five old assistant flags mapped to the three A25 ones).
4. `reason` arguments of staff actions have no column; they go to the console log and the audit columns (the S8c brief says so for resolve / rollback).
5. The implementer kept a throwaway smoke suite under `tmp/` (gitignored); nothing of it is in the repo.
6. Biome formatting not applied (lint gate is M3).
