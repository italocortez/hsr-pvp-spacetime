# S10g — tournament rulings and audit findings (2026-11-09)

## Goal

Implement the user's tournament rulings from the backend closing audit and the tournament findings that need no ruling. Rows A95 to A102, A106 and A107 are left for S10h.

## Decisions implemented

- **A94**: the organizer of an unfinished tournament cannot delete their own account; every deletion path settles the user's tournament ties first (withdrawn before the bracket exists, disqualified once it does) and removes their requests, assistant rows and stand-in approvals.
- **A103**: `isAnonymousDefault` changes in Draft only.
- **A104**: `lock_tournament_accounts`, until the player's own first game of the tournament; a waitlisted player's chosen accounts are remembered for promotion.
- **A105**: the seven staff views show unfinished tournaments to the organizer, assistants, Moderators and Admins; seven Admin-only views show any tournament by id.
- Audit findings without a decision row: tournament 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 11, 13, LOW 15, 16.

## Findings and hand-offs

- **Voided games and late locking** (fix pass): voiding deletes the participant and member rows, so a voided game cannot be tied to who played it. A Voided record with no participant rows counts for every member of the team that played the match. A member who sat out only a voided game is also locked out. An exact rule would need participant rows kept on void, or a schema change.
- **A waitlisted player who named no accounts and later locks one** gets only the chosen accounts at promotion, not all of them.
- **Guest linking Discord mid-tournament** (review F2): the link path deletes the old guest through `performUserDeletion`, so the guest's tournament entry is now withdrawn or disqualified. Entries were never moved to the linked account. Reported to the user; unchanged here.
- **Orchestrator readings accepted:** players awaiting approval may also add locks; an override stays possible on a staff decision that agrees with the games; Moderators see unfinished tournaments, Draft included, in the staff views; staff keep seeing waitlisted, unapproved and disqualified registrants in the registrant-accounts view.
- Any assistant row grants all seven staff views whatever its flags (unchanged, noted by the review).
- An admin or server deletion of an organizer still leaves `organizerId` pointing at the deleted user (A94 sets no rule for it).
- LOW findings left: tournament 17 to 24 (see the audit report). Late locking lets a player add a stronger account after seeing the bracket and before their first game: allowed by A104, pointed out to the user.
- Deviations from a decision row: none.
