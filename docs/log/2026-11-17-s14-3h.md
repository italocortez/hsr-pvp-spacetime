## Deviations and open points

- "Picking for slot N." was only visible before; it is now announced on the empty-slot press so the information is not lost.
- Open point: on a full team, a click on an unpicked card does nothing visible any more. Suggestions: unpicked cards look unavailable (dimmed, `aria-disabled` kept off so the announcement still runs) while the team is full; or a short shake or tint on the pressed card.
- Open point: the picture option's new label names Copy Image only, while it also changes the downloaded file.
- Old scratch scripts under `tmp/pw/s14-3d/`, `tmp/pw/s14-3d-review/` and `tmp/pw/s14-3f/` still find the checkbox by its old name; they are earlier steps' local files and were not changed.
- The recovered pool space (about 50px at 1440, 68px at 390) is worked out from the removed rules, not measured before the change.
