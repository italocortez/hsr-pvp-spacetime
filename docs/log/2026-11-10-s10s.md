## Findings and hand-offs

- **For S10t (A127)**: the second review maps what a bid and a sale write and what an exact undo must restore. Budgets cannot be restored from the AuctionSold step alone (the split of the eidolon extra between the two budgets is not logged); the last sale also moves the stage to Equipping and rolls budgets over.
- A referee who is also a seated captain can take back their own nomination before the other team bids; Classic already allows the same for a pick.
- The ban enforced at reconnect records the banned user as the job creator, so it neither hands over nor cancels (documented in `docs/auth/architecture.md`). Later fix: pass the ban's author.
- `run_user_deletion` has no caller guard and now reads the job creator to choose cancel or hand-over; the guard question is triage item L11 (S11).
- A121 side effects: the heir cannot delete their own account until the tournament ends; a banned user's Draft tournaments pass on too; "longest-serving" is the first assignment date; the acting staff member's role is read when the job runs, 5 s after the request.
- The membership allowance is shared by joins, seat moves, ready toggles, calendar and roster writes. Normal pages do not reach 20 per 10 s; scheduling more than 20 match events at once would, and would briefly block joining a lobby. Pages must pace bulk calls (M3 onward). `clearMembershipCount` in the bulk fixture means no calendar test shows the bulk limit.
- L71: per-view permissions to be decided with the staff page; `adminMatchTools.ts` still accepts any assistant row.
- L66: a stand-in whose own enrolment is disqualified can still be approved for another team (open with the user). In Seeding after generation the text "Bracket generated; use stand-ins." points at an action that needs the tournament in progress.
- L86: the clean-up covers the caller's own rows; an inactive organizer's old events stay in invitees' calendars.
- L100: the allow-list checks flags, not the command; a destructive positional is not reachable through the scripts.
- L101: a failed save leaves an orphan registered delegate; a failed revoke leaves the old delegate registered; a successful re-run still needs the Vercel token replaced.
- Comments at `userDeletion.ts:24` and `userDeletionHelper.ts:145` name two flag sites; there are three (to fix in S10t).
- Test gaps (second review N5): A125 with no bans, paused, at the cap, non-referee; A124 on a Draft tournament and the GC / link-provider negatives; two calendar reducers on an old target. For S10q.
- Deviations from a decision row: A126 withdraws a clause of A122(20) for A37 / A103, reported to the user.
