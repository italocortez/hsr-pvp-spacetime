## Choices made here

- **1120 fits:** the two cards stay side by side down to 1041 px (C stacked them at 1120). The Loadouts actions are then two rows of two, so Modes puts its cost set and draft mode on a row each and the cards come out level (276 px each for a visitor, 234 for a member). The slot cards end at 845 (visitor) and 803 (member), on the first 900 px screen. Below 1041 the cards stack.
- **The status place:** one line beside the hide option, shared by the "Copy link" confirmation and the picture's (both stay `role="status"` live regions, shown by opacity, as the copy-button guide says). An empty one leaves the flow (positioned, still in the accessibility tree), so whichever speaks has the line; two at once stack in the same column. A one-line confirmation moves nothing (measured: Copy link at 1440, roster top 390 before and after). A long one wraps and both cards grow by a line while it shows (Copy image or Download with "Some pictures could not be loaded and show as blanks." measured 248 px instead of 218 at 1440; with real images the lines are one line).
- **The sign-in line:** the full sentence, kept word for word; its first words are the link, and the separate "Sign in with Discord" link that followed the sentence is gone (same target, one link instead of two). One line at 1440 and 1920; two at 1120, 390 and 360. It is the card's last quiet row, after the notices, before "Browser storage is off" (the order the card had). A member has no line and the card is 27 px shorter; Modes measures the same then.
- **Notices:** each stays in its own card (no move across the reading order, so phones keep today's order). Whichever card grows, the other stretches with it: Modes spreads its rows, Loadouts keeps its rows together in the middle.
- **Modes when Loadouts is taller:** the body fills the card with `align-content: space-evenly`, so a visitor's extra line (27 px at 1440) becomes rhythm between the rows rather than a gap at the bottom.
- **Long names:** a 40-character name ends in an ellipsis in the field until it is focused; nothing else in the top row truncates at any measured width.
- **Touch:** on coarse pointers the name box, the four buttons, the option and the notice buttons stay 44 px; the sign-in link is inline text in a sentence (exempt from the target-size rule).
- Guides used: `ui-components/copy-button.md` (the live region shown by opacity, not covering content). Container queries per the card's own width (`css/size-aware-styling.md` not reread this step).
