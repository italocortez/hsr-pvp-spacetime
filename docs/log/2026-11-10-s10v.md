# S10v — tournament stand-ins: rules and the audited bugs (2026-11-10)

## Goal

Implement the user's stand-in rules (A135 to A138, A140) and fix the stand-in bugs of `notes/audit-2026-11-09/standin-audit.md` that needed no ruling. A139 and A141 were recorded as "leave as is".

## Decisions implemented

| Rule | As built |
|---|---|
| A135(1), A137(4) | A stand-in passes the registration requirements (verified account, active game account under `requireRoster` or `requireOwnership`, minimum rating); registration checks the game account under `requireOwnership` too |
| A135(2), A137(1), A137(2) | The organizer and every assistant are refused as stand-ins; nobody approves themself |
| A135(3) | A member of a team that is still in (Forming, Ready, Active) is refused; eliminated, withdrawn and non-enrolled users are accepted; a member of one team of a match never gets the other side |
| A135(5) | No cap on approvals; `start_draft` refuses a side whose seated players are all stand-ins; approval is refused in a solo tournament |
| A135(6) | New staff view `view_my_tournament_stand_ins` |
| A135(8) | `lock_tournament_accounts` accepts an approved stand-in within the A104 limit |
| A136 | Approval from bracket generation until the tournament ends, for an open match |
| A138 (replaces A135(4), A137(3)) | No automatic seat move. Seating into a free seat on join as before; a full side refuses; the host moves people by hand; a team captain may move a stand-in of their own team to Spectator |
| A140 | A stand-in approval does not allow creating the match lobby; a team member or a role (tournament staff with the bracket permission, Moderator, Admin) creates it |

Bugs closed (audit ids): SI1 (two sides of one's own team's match), SI2 (no locks for a stand-in), SI4 (a revoked stand-in kept the seat), SI5 (a disqualified stand-in kept the approval), SI12 (locks never released), SI13 (approval for a decided match or a bye), SI14 (a stale approval after a re-resolve), SI16 (the match missing from the stand-in's views and calendar), SI17 (A136), SI20 (stale docs).

## Review

Three independent Fable 5.2 reviews, read-only, each with its own probes (`notes/audit-2026-11-09/s10v-review.md`, `-2.md`, `-3.md`).

- Review 1, **COMMIT AFTER FIXES**: the eight audited bugs closed on the audit's own sequences. Gating: a roll-back made an eliminated stand-in's team active again while the approval stayed; stand-ins could push a match event over its 9-invite cap; a refusal text printed a private rating. Also: a stand-in later made assistant or organizer; a disqualified member keeping a Waiting seat; locking at approval locked users who had not agreed to anything.
- The orchestrator changed the lock moment from approval to the stand-in's first own action. Review 2, **COMMIT AFTER FIXES**, found the hole this opened (G1): a stand-in seated by the host was never locked and the game started, in three variants. Fixed by also taking the snapshot at `confirm_ready`, skipping it once the user has played, and deleting selections when stand-in locks are released.
- Review 3, **COMMIT**: every seating path locks and selects before a start; no start skips confirmations; no displacement code left. One gap (N1): in an unrated `requireOwnership` tournament a seated player without a selected account could start and the draft stalled. Fixed: `start_draft` requires a selected account from every seated player there.

A140 was added after review 3 and was verified by the orchestrator's suite run only; the implementer wrote its tests after the code.

## Findings and hand-offs

- Stand-in locks are told from an entrant's own locks by time, since no column records a lock's origin. Two bounded limits remain (a lock kept until the tournament ends when an earlier approval was revoked before a later one; an entrant's own later lock released at revoke). Candidate for S11: an origin column on `TournamentPlayerAccount`, and a `tournamentId` index (the end sweep scans the table).
- A Moderator or Admin who is an approved stand-in and creates the lobby through the role is its host and referee; `transfer_host`, `reclaim_referee` or other staff are the way out.
- A rolled-back or Rejected game no longer counts as "played" (older behaviour; a Rejected record is not treated like a Voided one).
- A roll-back that ends with the team eliminated again still removes its members' approvals.
- A stand-in left out of an event at the invite cap is not invited when room frees later; a stand-in also invited by hand loses that invite at revoke.
- `add_participant`, waitlist promotion, check-in and team requests do not check the game account requirement, as before for `requireRoster`.
- SI9 remains for eliminated players and outsiders: a stand-in sees the real names of the team it joins in an anonymous tournament.
- Not probed by the reviewers: group formats, the third-place path, user deletion.
- Deviations from a decision row: none.
