# S10ab — a referee seated on the other team cannot resume an automatic pause (2026-11-11)

## Goal

Close the case the second S10aa review found (F1): the 15 s check can move the referee flag to a seated opponent, who could then resume the dropped team's pause.

## Decisions implemented

- **A155**: on an automatic pause, a caller seated as a player on the team that does not own the pause is refused, with or without the referee flag.
- **A156**: recorded, no code change (the pause nobody online can lift is accepted).

## Files changed

- Module: `reducers/draft/draftControl.ts` (one condition in `resume_draft`: `isSeatedPlayer(member)` replaces `!isReferee` in the automatic-pause team check).
- Tests: 12 new in `match-session/reserve-time.test.ts`: 1v1 and 2v2 with a playing referee who drops, the forfeit claim after the grace, spectator and coach referees, Red as the paused team for the A153 cases, the manual pause rule with a seated referee. No existing test needed re-alignment.
- Docs: `docs/match-session/architecture.md`, `docs/match-session/contract.md` (one marker, two rows), `DECISIONS.md` (A155, A156), `ROADMAP.md`.
