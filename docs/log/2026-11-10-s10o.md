# S10o — random auto-pick without accounts, a fair timer draw, clean removal of a guest's entry (2026-11-10)

## Goal

Implement A117 (the random auto-pick needs accounts only where the lobby requires ownership; a re-rolled turn can land elsewhere) and A118 (staff remove a guest's approved entry without a disqualification).

## Decisions implemented

- **A117(1)**: `start_draft`'s `autoRandomPick` account check runs only when the lobby requires ownership.
- **A117(3)**: the timer draw changes after an undo; implemented as a well-mixed hash of the transaction time, the lobby, the turn and the next step sequence.
- **A118**: `reject_participant` also removes the approved entry of a guest before the bracket exists, as a withdrawal.
- A117(2) changes no code (pools unchanged). A119 (a guest who loses their browser loses the guest account) is recorded only.

## The draw

MurmurHash3 x86_32 over five 32-bit words (timestamp low, timestamp high, lobby id, turn index, next step sequence), then multiply-high reduction to the pool size (residual bias at most n / 2^32). Deterministic for a given transaction and state; a later transaction and the Undo row's sequence change it; a pool of one gives index 0. Measured over 200,000 inputs: n = 2: 49.956% to 50.044%; n = 12: 8.133% to 8.444% (ideal 8.333%); n = 80: 1.172% to 1.315% (ideal 1.25%).

## Review

Independent Fable 5.2 reviewer, read-only, ran the suites itself, verdict **COMMIT AFTER FIXES** (`scratchpad/s10o-review.md`). The behaviour met A117 and A118; nothing downstream assumes accounts when the auto-pick is on without ownership; the expiry reducers take only the lobby id, cannot be aimed or retried for a better result, and a player-referee re-rolling by undo gains nothing over a manual pick. Folded in by a fix pass:

- F1 (gating): the first version drew with `ctx.random`, whose host seed (derived from the transaction timestamp through a sign-filled 32-bit value) skews the first output: at n = 80 one position came up 4.1% of the time against an ideal 1.25%. Replaced by the hash above; a distribution test guards it.
- F2: a test now proves the draw depends on the transaction time, not only on the sequence.
- F3: A118 gaps covered (an already Disqualified guest row, a guest upgraded in place, removal in Seeding followed by bracket generation).

## Findings and hand-offs

- **Open for the user: three other places still use `ctx.random` and carry the same seed skew**: random bracket seeding (`bracketGeneration.ts`), the RandomSwap of `shuffle_teams` (`lobbySettings.ts`), and the random team formation of A36a (`tournamentHelpers.ts`). Recommendation put to the user: move them to the same mixed draw.
- Undo has no cap on its own rows (review F5, pre-existing); each re-roll costs a full turn timer, so this step adds no new growth path.
- A withdrawal does not promote the waitlist by itself; after a removal `waitlist_promote` succeeds.
- Calendar-invite deletion on an A118 removal is the withdrawal path's and is not asserted by a test.
- Deviations from a decision row: none.
